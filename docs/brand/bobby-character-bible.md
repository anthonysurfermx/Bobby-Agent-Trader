# Bobby Character Bible

## Purpose

Ten original, high-definition 3D characters for Bobby. They belong to the same
world: a social-finance intelligence universe that feels global, confident,
playful, and movement-first. They are designed for app-store imagery, social
video, onboarding, and real-time product states.

The desired motion language is expressive, polished, and game-like: clear
silhouettes, satisfying idle loops, emotes, and cosmetics. It must never copy
Fortnite, Epic Games assets, or any existing character design.

## Shared visual system

- **World:** dark obsidian (`#08090D`), electric blue (`#2F6BFF`), Bobby green
  (`#22C67A`), ultraviolet (`#7C52FF`), and occasional warm gold (`#E0A828`).
- **Materials:** matte technical fabric, smoked glass, soft-touch vinyl,
  brushed chrome, and restrained emissive accents.
- **Render:** high-end stylized 3D, cinematic global illumination, soft contact
  shadows, subtle depth of field, no plastic-looking AI artifacts.
- **Body language:** calm confidence and curiosity. No weapons, luxury logos,
  or “get rich” imagery.
- **Product role:** Bobby gives information and debate, not financial promises.

## 01 — Bobby Orb (the core mascot)

**Role:** the always-present intelligence companion and primary product mascot.

**Design:** a 34 cm floating obsidian sphere with a translucent emerald energy
core. A thin brushed-chrome orbital ring encircles it at an angle. The front
has two subtle LED apertures that can form minimal, readable expressions. A
small white `B` glyph appears only during an activated state.

**Personality:** precise, warm, lightly mischievous, never frantic.

**Signature motion:** slow hover with a three-degree tilt; ring precesses while
the core pulses. For analysis, it focuses a scanning beam. For a no-trade
verdict, the ring closes around the core like a protective shield.

**Palette:** obsidian, Bobby green, chrome, white micro-highlights.

**Generation prompt:** `Original premium 3D mascot, floating obsidian sphere with a translucent emerald energy core and one thin brushed-chrome orbital ring, minimal expressive LED eyes, dark studio background, green rim light, cinematic product render, high-detail materials, full body centered, no text, no brand logos, no existing game character references.`

## 02 — Byte (the voice guide)

**Role:** speaks, listens, and welcomes a new user into Bobby.

**Design:** compact humanoid robot with a softly rounded CRT-style visor head,
short black technical hoodie, graphite cargo shorts over fitted leggings,
oversized white-and-black sneakers, and a small glowing voice-wave module on
the chest.

**Personality:** social, observant, and good at explaining complicated things.

**Signature motion:** leans in to listen, taps the chest module to speak, and
projects a soft waveform between its hands.

**Palette:** graphite, black, white, electric blue, green display details.

**Generation prompt:** `Original stylized 3D companion robot, compact proportions, rounded dark visor face with expressive green pixel eyes, black technical hoodie, graphite cargo shorts, clean chunky sneakers, glowing voice waveform chest module, premium collectible toy meets cinematic game character, full body, dark neutral studio, no text, no logos.`

## 03 — Flux (the signal scout)

**Role:** finds useful movement amid noisy market information.

**Design:** a fast, fox-like digital creature with midnight-black soft-touch
fur, tapered ears that illuminate in green, and a tail made of segmented
floating light bars. It wears a tiny black utility harness rather than clothes.

**Personality:** alert, nimble, inquisitive.

**Signature motion:** ear flick, short pounce, tail drawing a smooth upward or
downward data path in the air.

**Palette:** black, jade green, mist gray, subtle ultraviolet tail edge.

**Generation prompt:** `Original stylized 3D fox-like digital mascot, midnight black soft-touch fur, luminous jade inner ears, segmented floating light tail that suggests data, minimal black utility harness, clever and alert pose, premium cinematic stylized render, full body, dark studio, no text, no logos.`

## 04 — Glitch (the contrarian)

**Role:** visual face of the Red Team; asks what could be wrong.

**Design:** a small asymmetrical creature formed from faceted smoked-glass
blocks. One floating eye is amber and the other is violet. Its body has a tiny
cropped bomber jacket and exaggerated rounded boots.

**Personality:** skeptical, funny, incisive; never hostile.

**Signature motion:** cocks its head, pulls a floating red flag from a pixel
tear, then dissolves it into a question mark.

**Palette:** smoked black, ultraviolet, amber, restrained red for warnings.

**Generation prompt:** `Original stylized 3D contrarian mascot made of asymmetrical smoked-glass geometric blocks, one amber floating eye and one ultraviolet eye, miniature black bomber jacket, rounded boots, clever skeptical expression, collectible art toy with cinematic game-quality materials, dark studio, no text, no logos.`

## 05 — Halo (the risk guardian)

**Role:** embodies discipline, limits, and the no-trade decision.

**Design:** a soft hovering pearl-white ring with a dark central aperture and
three tiny green lights. When activated it expands into a translucent shield
plane. No face; it communicates through breathing-like light patterns.

**Personality:** serene, firm, protective.

**Signature motion:** gentle orbit, then a decisive snap into shield position.

**Palette:** pearl white, chrome, green, faint ice blue.

**Generation prompt:** `Original premium 3D risk guardian mascot, minimal floating pearl-white halo ring around a dark central aperture, three small emerald indicator lights, translucent energy shield emerging from ring, serene futuristic product character, cinematic studio lighting, no text, no logos.`

## 06 — Momo (the curious analyst)

**Role:** makes exploration, questions, and learning feel playful.

**Design:** a translucent jelly-like character with a rounded square body,
floating tiny hands, large liquid-black eyes, and a small internal violet-green
gradient. It wears one single chrome ear cuff.

**Personality:** curious, emotionally expressive, optimistic.

**Signature motion:** squashes into a magnifying glass shape, stretches to
point at data, then re-forms with a bubble pop.

**Palette:** clear glass, ultraviolet, Bobby green, chrome.

**Generation prompt:** `Original cute premium 3D jelly mascot, translucent rounded-square body, floating tiny hands, expressive liquid-black eyes, internal violet to emerald glow, one chrome ear cuff, stylish collectible character, high-definition cinematic render, dark studio background, no text, no logos.`

## 07 — Rook (the strategist)

**Role:** makes a complex thesis feel considered, calm, and structured.

**Design:** a compact raven-inspired mechanical character with smooth folded
wing panels, a faceted black helmet-like head, and a small glowing chess-rook
emblem on its chest. It stands on two slim articulated legs.

**Personality:** quiet, strategic, patient.

**Signature motion:** unfolds one wing into a floating plan grid, studies it,
then folds it back with a nod.

**Palette:** matte black, gunmetal, green, a minimal gold detail.

**Generation prompt:** `Original stylized premium 3D strategic bird mascot, compact raven-inspired mechanical body, matte black folded wing panels, faceted gunmetal head, tiny emerald rook-like chest glyph, articulate legs, calm intelligent pose, cinematic collectible game-character render, no text, no logos.`

## 08 — Zip (the city messenger)

**Role:** carries updates across CDMX, NYC, and Seoul.

**Design:** an androgynous mini courier with a reflective oversized jacket,
rounded helmet with a single green visor stripe, cross-body messenger bag, and
magnetic hover sneakers. Their silhouette is human but toy-like, with no real
person’s likeness.

**Personality:** energetic, dependable, globally fluent.

**Signature motion:** lands from a short hover, slides a data card from the bag,
and points the visor toward the user.

**Palette:** black, silver, electric blue, locally variable accent color.

**Generation prompt:** `Original stylized 3D urban courier mascot, androgynous compact toy-like proportions, reflective oversized black technical jacket, round helmet with one glowing green visor stripe, cross-body data messenger bag, magnetic hover sneakers, premium cinematic game character, full body, no text, no logos.`

## 09 — Kora (the community connector)

**Role:** represents shared ideas and a conversation that is bigger than one
screen.

**Design:** a smooth ceramic-like creature with long antennae and a simple face
display. Its torso is a rounded dark pod, while six soft cables terminate in
small glowing nodes. It can plug nodes into space, not devices.

**Personality:** inviting, collaborative, composed.

**Signature motion:** spins a web of three colored nodes, connecting them into
a single bright conclusion.

**Palette:** deep navy, bone ceramic, green, violet, warm gold nodes.

**Generation prompt:** `Original high-end stylized 3D community mascot, rounded deep navy pod body with ceramic upper shell, two long elegant antennae, simple friendly face display, six soft cable tendrils ending in small glowing nodes, futuristic collectible design, cinematic dark studio, no text, no logos.`

## 10 — Axiom (the record keeper)

**Role:** protects the immutable record of calls, outcomes, and lessons.

**Design:** a small levitating archive creature: a tall rounded black monolith
with a green vertical light seam, thin floating pages of glass orbiting it, and
two simple eyes recessed into its upper face.

**Personality:** impartial, grounded, trustworthy.

**Signature motion:** receives a glowing data point, stamps it into a glass
page, and adds the page to its orbit.

**Palette:** obsidian, emerald, translucent smoke glass, white.

**Generation prompt:** `Original premium 3D archive mascot, small floating rounded obsidian monolith with a vertical emerald light seam, two subtle recessed eyes, thin translucent smoked-glass record pages orbiting around it, calm authoritative expression, cinematic high-definition product render, no text, no logos.`

## Production sequence

1. Create a model sheet for **Bobby Orb**, **Byte**, and **Glitch** first.
2. Lock a shared material library and proportion guide.
3. Rig each character with idle, greeting, thinking, pointing, celebrating,
   caution, and exit motion states.
4. Build regional cosmetic packs — CDMX, NYC, and Seoul — without changing
   each character's core silhouette.
