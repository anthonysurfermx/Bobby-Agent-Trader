## Full description (4000 max)

```
Most market apps hand you an answer. Bobby makes the answer survive an argument first.

Ask about any market — out loud or by typing. Bitcoin, NVIDIA, gold, and six hundred more.
Three agents then take it apart in front of you:

• ALPHA HUNTER builds the case for the trade.
• RED TEAM attacks it and tries to break it.
• The CIO rules, and has to explain the ruling.

Sometimes the ruling is NO TRADE. That is a real answer here, not a failure — when nothing
survives the debate, Bobby says so and tells you what it was watching instead.

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY GET

A desk, not a chatbot. Live prices, candles and market structure, read by companions who
each explain the same data differently — plain speak, no-fluff, or full trading-desk
technical. Pick the one you can stand listening to.

Discipline, not volume. XP comes from reading the analysis and coming back, never from
trading more and never from spending money. Gear unlocks as you go and changes how your
companion works: a radar that watches live data, a shield that blocks setups with no edge,
a translator that turns "RSI divergence" into something you would say to a friend.

Trader Land. Good calls build an island that is yours — a small world that grows from the
decisions you got right, and that you can show other people.

A public record. Bobby's own calls are written down before the market settles them, and
anyone can check them afterwards. A track record, not a promise.

Español y English. Every screen, both languages, switchable at any time.

ON-CHAIN, ON YOUR TERMS

Bobby can prepare a Base swap for a wallet you control. You review it and you sign it in
your own wallet. Bobby never holds your funds, never holds your keys, and never signs
anything for you. If you never connect a wallet, everything else still works.

WHAT BOBBY IS NOT

Bobby is not a broker, not a fund, not a signal service, and not your financial adviser.
Nothing in this app is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything, and none of it is
tailored to you or your situation. It is educational market analysis produced by software,
and software gets things wrong. Market data can be delayed or incorrect. Markets can move
against you and you can lose money. Every decision is yours, and so is every result. If
you want advice, talk to a licensed professional.

18+.
```

## Notes for whoever fills the form

- Keep the "WHAT BOBBY IS NOT" block. It is the same text the app shows on its first
  screen, and a reviewer who sees the listing and the app say the same thing has one less
  reason to look harder.
- Do not add performance numbers, win rates or anything that reads as a return. Play's
  misrepresentation policy is where finance apps get removed, and 75% on n=4 is not a
  claim worth making.
- Category: **Finance**. Tags: finance, education. Not "Games", despite Trader Land —
  Games brings the Families policy surface with it for nothing.
