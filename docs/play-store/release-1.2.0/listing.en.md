## Title

Bobby: Market Perspectives

## Short description

Explore market evidence with three AI perspectives. Learn at your own pace.

## Full description

Meet Bobby, your companion for thinking through the market.

Ask about a supported stock or crypto asset by typing or optional on-device dictation. Three AI perspectives explore the evidence: an opportunity view, a risk challenge and a synthesis. Read the reasoning, a price chart and source timestamps. A Wait result is useful when evidence is limited.

Keep your own theses on this phone: what you are watching, why and what could change your mind. A review sends your words with that request and uses a read. Credits shows the allowances the service has confirmed; Quick, Deep and Max have different limits.

Memory is optional. Its consent explains what an account remembers and what this phone keeps. You can view and erase that information. Choose local thesis reminders and optional follow-ups; Android may delay notifications. Follow-ups can be stopped from their notification or in Reminders.

Use Bobby in English, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian or German. Choose your companion and interface language in Profile. Guest reads have a limited allowance; an account is optional for the first reads. Internet is required for market data and AI responses. Dictation requires an available on-device Android speech service; typing is always available.

You choose whether Bobby can send questions and narration text for external AI processing. Review the risk notice before your first read. You can report an AI answer inside the app, and request account deletion from Profile or the support website.

Bobby is an educational market tool. Data may be delayed and AI responses may be incomplete or incorrect. The Android app does not connect a wallet, place orders or move money. There are no guaranteed outcomes.
