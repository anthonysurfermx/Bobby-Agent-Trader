# Script v1 — Bobby promo (motion graphics, 15 s, 9:16 first)

**Status:** draft for Anthony's approval · 2026-09-03 · objective: announce the app waitlist (everything centred on the app)
**Core message:** ES "ChatGPT te responde. Bobby comprueba el mercado antes de responder." · EN "ChatGPT answers. Bobby checks the market first."

## Principle

The whole film is the core sentence, split in two and acted out. First half (2 s): ChatGPT answers
instantly. Second half (10 s): Bobby is asked the same thing, holds the answer, checks the market,
and only then answers. Brand frame closes. The market check is SHOWN (panels, scan, one red flag,
a shield) but never NAMED (no debate / agents / verdict / record on screen).

Style: motion graphics inside the obsidian terminal world (near-black #08090D, thin grid, smoked
glass panels, chrome bevels, Bobby green #22C67A as the only emissive accent). Kinetic type in
screen space. One character only: Byte, in shot 5, as the face of the answer. Halo appears as
an object (pearl ring → shield), not as a named character. Real screenshots are used as style
references for grid, glass and monospace labels; never redrawn.

## Why a "no trade" answer

Showing Bobby say "not yet" is the proof of the sentence: an AI that checks first is one that can
say no. A bullish answer would make the film look like every other crypto ad and would promise
something. Brand rule since v1: "it's allowed to say no."

## Open decisions for Anthony

1. Pure motion graphics (no characters at all) vs. Byte cameo in shots 5–6 as drafted. Byte gives
   the film a face and links it to the app's mascots; removing it makes it cleaner but colder.
2. The answer shown in shot 4: "Todavía no hay trade." (drafted) vs. a neutral read like
   "Espera la confirmación en 4H."
3. Language per format: es-MX for Reels/TikTok and EN for LinkedIn/X (drafted), or EN everywhere.
