# The dial: how Bobby speaks to you

Brief for the native build (iPhone and Android, build 68). Owner's decision of 2026-10-09: proposal B, "el dial".
Product lead: Claude. Native: Codex. Server: done on this branch (PR #169).

## Why

Bobby is for people who have never invested, and today it speaks to everyone like a trader. Ten real Quick reads
on 2026-10-09 (guest, es and en, a newcomer's questions): chart words in the first four lines 10 of 10, 4.5 per
read; none said what the asset is. The owner wants Bobby to ask first how to speak, without losing what works
today: straight to Bobby, value before any account, everything on the sphere.

## What it is

One moment before the first question, on the home, around the sphere. No new screen, no form, no account.

1. Bobby says one line, by voice and as the page's eyebrow: **"¿Cómo quieres que te hable?"** / "How should I talk to you?"
2. An arc under the sphere with three stops and a thumb. Labels under the arc: **Sencillo · Con términos · Técnico**
   (en: Plain · With terms · Technical). The thumb starts on Sencillo.
3. As the thumb moves, two things change at once:
   - the sample sentence under the arc (fixed copy below, the same made-up situation in the three wordings);
   - the sphere: `idle` on Sencillo, `listen` on Con términos, `debate` on Técnico. Tint and mode only, through
     sphere.js. The sphere is never replaced and nothing is drawn inside the glass.
4. One button: **"Así está bien"** / "That works". It saves the choice and hands over to the normal home, where
   Bobby asks **"¿Qué quieres entender hoy?"** / "What do you want to understand today?".
5. A quiet text button at the bottom: **"Empezar ahora"** / "Start now". Same as accepting Sencillo.

Ten seconds, one gesture. A person who does nothing and taps "Empezar ahora" gets plain words.

### Sample sentences (fixed copy, not a model call; a made-up situation, no real asset or figure)

| | es | en |
|---|---|---|
| Sencillo | Subió muy rápido en las últimas horas. Hay un techo cerca, donde antes dejó de subir. | It rose fast in the last hours. There is a ceiling nearby, where it stopped rising before. |
| Con términos | Subió muy rápido (sobrecompra). Hay un techo cerca, donde antes dejó de subir (resistencia). | It rose fast (overbought). There is a ceiling nearby, where it stopped rising before (resistance). |
| Técnico | Sobrecomprado en 1H, bajo resistencia. Falta confirmación. | Overbought on 1H, under resistance. No confirmation yet. |

fr, pt, it, de: translate these six lines; keep them under 90 characters.

## When it shows

- First launch after the risk notice, before the first question. Once.
- Never again by itself. It lives in Memory as one row, **"Cómo te habla Bobby"**, which opens the same dial.
- A person updating from 1.8 does not get it at launch. They keep today's behaviour (see the contract) and find
  the row in Memory. One nudge on the glass, once, may point at it ("Ahora eliges cómo te hablo").
- After the third read, once: the chip row offers **"Más sencillo · Así está bien · Más técnico"**. A tap moves the
  dial one stop. Never asked again after an answer.

## The contract (server, live once PR #169 is deployed)

`POST /api/desk-debate` takes one more optional field:

```json
{ "symbol": "MU", "question": "...", "language": "es", "speech": "plain" }
```

- `speech`: `"plain"` | `"terms"` | `"technical"`. Send it on every read once the person has chosen.
- Omitted: the server uses the account's experience ("experienced" reads technical) and otherwise plain. So a
  1.8 client, which sends nothing, now reads plain words. That is intended.
- An unknown value is ignored, never a 400.
- It changes the CIO's wording only. Verdict, direction, sufficiency, levels and the next question do not depend
  on it. Alpha and Red Team keep their vocabulary.

Store the choice on the phone, per reader like the ledger (signed out included). It is not sent anywhere else.
Moving it to the account so both phones agree is the shared-memory work, not this build.

## Not in this build

- The other two questions (what brings you here; days, months or years). They come later, one at a time, after a
  read, in the chip row. Horizon first: it is what lets Quick stop answering "not enough data".
- Any question about money, income or risk tolerance. Never: it turns Bobby into a suitability check.

## Done means

- A new install reaches its first answer in at most one extra tap ("Empezar ahora").
- The thumb can be dragged and each label tapped; VoiceOver/TalkBack reads it as an adjustable with three values.
- The sample and the sphere change within one frame of the thumb; reduced motion keeps the change, drops the tween.
- The chosen value is in the next read's request body, and survives relaunch and sign-in.
- The same pictures in es and en on both phones, light text budget respected (V18-DESIGN.md).
- Nothing about the dial is drawn inside the sphere's glass; no avatar there either.
