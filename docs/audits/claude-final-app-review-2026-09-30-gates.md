## privacy-backend

No blocker found in privacy-backend. The live policy text matches the audited source (52d28b3) and the code largely backs its claims: questions are never written to Bobby's database, IPs are stored only as salted hashes, the 35-day read cleanup and account-deletion FK cascades exist in the prod database, iOS payloads to OpenAI/Anthropic carry no account identifiers, and the archive has no analytics SDK.
