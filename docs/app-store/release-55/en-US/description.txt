Meet Bobby, your companion for thinking through the market.

Ask about a supported asset, such as a stock or crypto asset, by typing or using optional dictation. Bobby brings three perspectives to the discussion: an opportunity view, a risk challenge and a synthesis of the evidence.

SIX LANGUAGES, LOCAL MARKET CONTEXT
Use Bobby in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian or German. Change the language in Profile. Portuguese voice and dictation follow the device's Portugal or Brazil language setting. Search supported local stocks by name or ticker; original exchange symbols and quote currencies are retained.

A READING YOU CAN REVISIT
See market context, a chart, source timestamps and a conditional conclusion. Save a reading on your device and return to its reasoning later. A Wait result is a useful answer when the evidence is not sufficient.

CHOOSE THE DEPTH
Quick, Deep and Max offer different analysis depths. Guest readings and signed-in levels have usage limits; availability and remaining allowances appear in the app. Some levels require Sign in with Apple.

YOUR COMPANION, YOUR PROGRESS
Choose a companion, customize your profile and explore Trader Land. An optional Apple account synchronizes progress. Your island stays private until you choose to publish it. Public islands include reporting and creator blocking.

YOUR CHOICE ABOUT AI
External AI processing requires your permission. You can review the notice and withdraw permission from Profile. Dictation is recognized on your iPhone when the language is installed; otherwise Apple's speech service transcribes it. Bobby does not store the audio. You can always type instead. Optional narration can be muted.

Bobby is a market analysis and educational tool. The iPhone app does not connect wallets, execute trades or move funds. Analyses may be incomplete or incorrect, and market data may be delayed. Nothing in Bobby is a promise of returns or personalized investment advice. You decide what to do.

BOBBY PRO (OPTIONAL)
Bobby Pro is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription: unlimited Quick reads (fair use) plus 60 Deep and 10 Max analyses every 30 days. The price is shown in your currency before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is in Profile.
