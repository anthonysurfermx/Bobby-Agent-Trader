# Bobby iOS — App Store screenshot storyboard v3

Target: iPhone portrait, 1284 × 2778 px, English (US).

## Act 1 — More than a chatbot

1. **BOBBY DOESN'T JUST ANSWER. IT DEBATES.**
   - Three specialized agents challenge every market idea before the verdict.
   - Byte appears inside the real Live Desk UI.

2. **SEE WHAT THE MARKET IS DOING RIGHT NOW.**
   - Live prices, market context and risk levels replace generic explanations.
   - Kora appears inside the real Live Desk UI.

3. **WHEN THERE IS NO EDGE, BOBBY SAYS SO.**
   - A NO TRADE verdict explains why waiting can protect capital.
   - Glitch appears inside the real Live Desk UI.

## Act 2 — The Bobby experience

4. **CHOOSE A COMPANION THAT SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE.**
   - The voice and personality change; the quality of the analysis does not.

5. **WAITING CAN BE A WIN.**
   - Protecting capital earns Discipline XP because good process matters.

6. **LEARN TO READ THE MARKET, NOT CHASE IT.**
   - Every analysis shows what confirms the thesis and what would break it.

7. **YOUR STREAK REWARDS BETTER DECISIONS.**
   - Opening the app earns nothing. Following the process does.

8. **YOUR COMPANION EVOLVES AS YOU DO.**
   - Progress comes from useful behavior, never from spending more.

9. **CHOOSE A 3D COMPANION THAT FEELS LIKE YOURS.**
   - The analysis stays serious while the experience stays playful.

10. **SERIOUS FINANCE WITHOUT THE BANKING VIBE.**
    - It feels like a game, but it thinks like a market desk.

## Creative rule

The first three frames establish the product difference with real app UI and three
distinct companions. Frames four through ten remove the device and sell the emotional
experience through complete, conversational statements rather than isolated feature words.
