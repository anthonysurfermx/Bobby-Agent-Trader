Bobby 1.5 (52) replaces rejected build 38. Guideline 5 / China mainland: China mainland is excluded from Availability (174 storefronts available, 1 unavailable) and from the subscription's availability.

Bobby provides educational stock and crypto market analysis. It does not execute trades, connect wallets, hold funds or private keys. No account or purchase is needed to try it: a new installation gets three Quick reads as a guest; Sign in with Apple then gives 10 reads per week.

IN-APP PURCHASE (new in this build): Bobby Pro Monthly (xyz.bobbyprotocol.bobby.pro.monthly), an optional auto-renewable subscription. To find it without using up the free reads: tap the companion avatar (top right) to open Profile → "Bobby Pro". The paywall shows the localized price and period, auto-renewal terms, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links, and Restore Purchases. "Restore Purchases" is also a row in Profile. Purchases are tied to the Bobby account, so the paywall asks to Sign in with Apple first if needed; use your Sandbox Apple Account for the purchase. Purchases are validated server-side through RevenueCat.

Review steps:
1. Tap the question button to type; hold it for optional on-device dictation. Declining microphone/speech permission keeps typing available.
2. Read the AI/risk notice and accept by holding "Hold to agree" (VoiceOver/keyboard can activate the consent button). Questions and public market context are sent to Anthropic (Claude) for analysis; if Anthropic is unavailable or out of credit, OpenAI performs the same roles automatically. Account identifiers and microphone audio are never in that payload.
3. Ask about Bitcoin or a supported stock (e.g. "Is NVDA worth a look this week?"). The sphere shows the analysis, chart, data timestamp and an educational Wait/Review outcome (never buy/sell). Pull down for the full three-agent debate.
4. Profile → Bobby Pro / Restore Purchases (above). Profile also has Risk notice (withdraw AI consent), mute narration, Sign out and Delete account. If a Bobby Pro subscription is active, the deletion screen explains that Apple keeps billing until it is canceled and offers Manage subscription. Deletion removes the account, synced progress and Trader Land; if automatic Sign in with Apple revocation is unavailable the app shows Apple's manual steps.
5. Trader Land publication is optional. Names are checked by moderation (fails closed); public islands offer Report and block creator; every report notifies the developer by email for review within 24 hours.

The app interface is English/Spanish; the Portuguese description states this. Editorial screenshots are lifestyle photographs with captions; they do not depict customers.
