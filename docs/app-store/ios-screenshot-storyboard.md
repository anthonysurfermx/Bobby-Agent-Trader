# Bobby iOS — App Store screenshot storyboard

Target: iPhone portrait, 1284 x 2778 px.  
Audience: Gen Z, es-MX.  
Visual thesis: lifestyle campaign photography + real Bobby UI + 3D companion energy. Serious finance, never legacy-fintech.

## Visual system

- One short headline per frame, 3–7 words.
- Real UI remains readable and visually dominant.
- Lifestyle layer supplies identity and emotion; it must never fabricate financial data.
- Dark graphite base, electric blue, Bobby green, selective violet/amber character accents.
- Large rounded grotesk headline; mono labels only inside the product UI.
- No fake Apple hardware, logos, exchange logos, returns, profit claims, or trading execution claims.

## Sequence

1. **TU MERCADO. TU BOBBY.**
   - Product: Live Desk hero and voice orb/companion.
   - Lifestyle: CDMX golden-hour creative walking through the city.
   - Job: immediate identity and voice-first differentiation.

2. **HÁBLALE AL MERCADO.**
   - Product: microphone/listening state with live transcript.
   - Lifestyle: late-night bedroom/studio, headphones, intimate bestie energy.
   - Job: show voice as the fastest entry point.

3. **3 AGENTES. 1 VEREDICTO.**
   - Product: Alpha Hunter → Red Team → CIO debate rail.
   - Lifestyle: NYC after rain, cinematic blue reflections.
   - Job: explain the core product architecture without technical overload.

4. **TU TESIS, BAJO PRESIÓN.**
   - Product: Red Team challenge state.
   - Lifestyle: bold violet/red editorial lighting, Glitch energy.
   - Job: make adversarial review feel valuable and fun.

5. **NO TRADE TAMBIÉN GANA.**
   - Product: guarded/no-setup verdict and risk boundary.
   - Lifestyle: Halo shield mood, calm rather than celebration of risk.
   - Job: establish trust and Bobby's signature discipline moment.

6. **VE MÁS QUE EL PRECIO.**
   - Product: live chart, levels, risk and invalidation.
   - Lifestyle: Seoul glass-and-neon city geometry.
   - Job: communicate serious market intelligence.

7. **RECUERDA LO QUE SIGUES.**
   - Product: personalized Quick Access and previous-asset recap.
   - Lifestyle: everyday coffee/transit ritual.
   - Job: show useful memory without surveillance vibes.

8. **FORJA TU PROPIA AURA.**
   - Product: Aura onboarding and share card.
   - Lifestyle: saturated customizable colored light.
   - Job: show identity, personalization and shareability.

9. **ELIGE TU SQUAD EN 3D.**
   - Product: native SceneKit gallery with character rotation.
   - Lifestyle: premium toy-store/game-character reveal.
   - Job: deliver the merch-not-fintech moment.

10. **FINANZAS SERIAS. CERO VIBRA BANCARIA.**
    - Product: desk overview with verdict, chart and companion.
    - Lifestyle: global Gen Z trio, CDMX/NYC/Seoul visual echoes.
    - Job: close with the complete brand promise.

## Production layers per image

1. Generated lifestyle background plate without text or phone UI.
2. Real simulator screenshot captured from the current iOS build.
3. Deterministic headline and device/window framing.
4. Final App Store export at 1284 x 2778 px, sRGB PNG, no alpha.
