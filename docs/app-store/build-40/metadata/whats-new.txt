Bobby 1.5 is rebuilt around a single glass sphere.

• Ask your way: hold the button and talk, or tap it and type. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone, and the microphone listens only while you hold.
• Watch the debate: Alpha Hunter, Red Team and the CIO argue inside the sphere before any answer appears.
• See the evidence: price, RSI, volume and the recent range orbit the sphere, next to a one-hour chart with support and resistance.
• One clear word: every read ends with Wait or Review, marked as an educational read, not financial advice.
• Pull down for the full read: the whole debate and a thesis card. Save the thesis to earn discipline XP.
• Swipe the sphere to visit your Squad and your saved theses.
• Tap your companion in the top corner for your account: sign in with Apple, sign out, delete your account, or open the privacy policy.
