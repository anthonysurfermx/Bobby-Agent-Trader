# Bobby Protocol — brand positioning

## Position

Bobby is the accountability layer for autonomous finance.

Autonomous agents can generate ideas quickly, but speed is not a safety model. Bobby makes a decision earn the right to move capital: a thesis is proposed, attacked by a Red Team, reconciled by a CIO and risk gate, then committed to a verifiable record before the outcome is known.

## Core message

**Before capital moves, make the thesis earn it.**

Supporting line: **Three agents debate. Risk gets a veto. The decision leaves a proof trail.**

## Audience

- **Agent builders:** a decision and verification layer they can call over MCP/API.
- **Capital operators:** a human-readable war room for inspecting conviction, downside and invalidation.
- **Protocol teams:** accountable, inspectable decision infrastructure instead of an opaque AI signal feed.

## Category and differentiation

Do not position Bobby as another AI trading bot, copy-trading marketplace or generic Base agent. Those products compete on execution, automation, leverage or distribution.

Bobby competes on **decision accountability**:

1. adversarial debate before execution;
2. explicit risk gates and invalidation;
3. commitment before outcome, without hindsight editing;
4. on-chain evidence and a public record;
5. an agent-native interface through MCP and paid protocol calls.

## Message rules

- Say **proof trail**, **committed record** or **inspectable decision**; do not say “guaranteed truth” or “impossible to manipulate.”
- Say **decision layer** or **accountability layer**; do not lead with “AI trading bot.”
- Say **Base-native build, with X Layer track record** while the migration is in progress.
- Keep the distinction between **on-chain commitment** and **market-price verification** explicit until TrackRecord v2 ships.
- Never use a small win rate as the headline proof of skill. Show counts and sample size.

## Voice

Precise, adversarial, calm and technical. Bobby is confident about process, cautious about outcomes and direct about residual risk.
